## Phase 4, batch 2 — the Manager's Today and Reports (2026-09-17, deployed 22:21 IST)

Scope: the company dashboard (Today for Manager/Admin), Reports, the team table, the ageing view, the drill from a number to its accounts. No financial formula, business rule, permission, schema or backend change; every count and amount is computed by the same code as before (`boxMetrics`, `crmPerformanceStats`, `portfolioAgeing`, `overdueAgeing`).

**What was confusing for a manager, found at 1366×768 with the live data (642 accounts with dues):**

- **Reports was a different app.** Four pink "instant" cards, a "Portfolio Overview & Ageing Summary" block with six tiles, then rainbow category chips — three summaries of the same numbers in a colour language nothing else uses, before the list started. The list itself rendered all 642 rows at once (a 58,000 px page).
- **Different words for the same thing.** The book says *Upcoming*, *No follow-up*, *Follow up*; Reports said *Future*, *No Date*, *Not Set*, *Update*, "Select CRM Owner:", ">90d Report", "Instant Report of >135d".
- **A separate search.** Reports had its own box; the app bar's term (Ctrl+K) did nothing there.
- **Today's numbers were dead ends.** The team table sat at the bottom of a long page with no way from a person's *Unattended 50* to those fifty accounts; the ageing bands were figures only; the worklist subtitle said "open it in the customer book" while the cards opened Reports.
- **Filters leaked.** Arriving in Reports from a card kept whatever ageing band or person was chosen last time, so the list under "Overdue" could silently be "Overdue · 91–135 days · Savia".

**What changed:**

- **Reports, top to bottom, in the book's chrome:** one filter card (CRM owner · With dues/Settled/All · Search, with *AI summary* and *Excel* as quiet buttons) → four attention tiles (Overdue, Due today, No follow-up, Bad debt — each is the filter it names; press it, the list is that list; press again, back to All) → one compact ageing card (the bar, then Outstanding + timely score and the four bands, each a filter) → the list, headed "*N* accounts", with the follow-up-state chips (All · Overdue · Due today · Upcoming · No follow-up · Unattended · Completed · Bad debt) and the ageing chips (All ageing · 1–45d · 46–90d · 91–135d · >135d · >90d total) in the book's colours. *Reset* clears state, ageing and search together. A *Needs attention* chip appears only while the banner's list is open, so a manager always sees which list they are looking at.
- **The rows:** *Customer · Balance · Ageing (bar; figures at xl) · Over 90 days · Follow-up / Status (the same `StatusBadge` and the same "3d overdue · 16 Sept" wording as the book, "₹11,953 expected") · CRM owner · Last note (xl) · WhatsApp + **Follow up***. Fifty rows at a time, *Show more — n left*. The name opens the account, as in the book. Fits 1024 px without sideways scroll.
- **One search:** the Reports box and the app bar's edit the same term; Ctrl+K focuses it here too.
- **Today, in the order a manager reads it:** Worklist (Overdue · Due today · No follow-up · Upcoming, + the bad-debt strip) → **Team** → Portfolio ageing → Cheques / Commitments. The team table is most-unattended first; its columns are *Accounts with dues · Completed · Due today · Overdue · Unattended · Timely score* (hover for what each means); a CRM's name and every count is a button that opens Reports on that person and that list (Accounts → all, Completed → completed, Due today → today, Overdue → overdue, Unattended → overdue-or-no-follow-up). A collector's row stays plain numbers: Reports filters by CRM owner and has no filter for accounts handed to a collector. Each ageing band and the *Past 45 / Past 90 days* figures open Reports on that band.
- **`openReport(...)`** in `App.tsx` is the one way from Today into Reports: it sets the person, the state and the band together (unset = none), so nothing from an earlier visit carries over; the banner's *Show them* goes through it too.
- **`unattended`** joined `FollowUpCategoryFilter` (overdue or no follow-up, never bad debt) so the team table's column and the Reports chip are the same set; the numbers agree (Savia: Unattended 50 = Overdue 41 + No follow-up 9).
- **Vocabulary** the same in Today, the team table, Reports and the book; `followUpWhen` moved to `components/ui/format.ts` so both views read it from one place; the book's "All Ageing" chip is "All ageing".

**Owner's decisions (same day):** no collector filter in Reports yet (T50 stays documented); the team table stays most-unattended first; and **T51 fixed** — a CRM's *Accounts with dues* count opens Reports on *With dues, not bad debt (n)*, a chip shown only while that list is open, so it lists exactly the accounts it counted; the "+n bad debt" line beneath the count is unchanged and the *Bad debt* chip/tile still carry them. For the same reason Reports' *Completed* no longer includes a defaulter (they were already out of every other follow-up list; production has 0 such accounts today). The name, which is not a count, opens the person's whole book with the *Bad debt* badges. The "Timely score" formula is unchanged and now has a tooltip. - **The customer book.** The desktop toolbar card is gone below `md`; its buttons live in one phone row under the search (*Filters · Totals · +*). **Three fixes after the owner's review (2026-09-19):**

1. **No actionable control on a phone is under 40 px any more** — 28 were left after the first pass, and the sweep now counts **0** across all 22 screens and dialogs: *Refresh* and *Retry now* in the status line, the team card's name and its four counts on Today, the book's *More filters*, *Edit details* and the designation field in the follow-up sheet, the alerts switch and its tick box, the cheque card's customer name, and the clear-search buttons in the app bar, Reports and Live stock.
2. **The report's ageing strip no longer cuts its small print.** A phone gets the shorter line — "644 accounts", "302 accounts" — instead of "644 accounts · timely score 94 %" and "27 % of the book · 302 accounts" ending in an ellipsis. The score is on the tiles above and the share is what the bar draws, so nothing is lost; the line is rendered short rather than hidden, so the laptop's DOM is unchanged.
3. **The team page no longer scrolls 6 px sideways at 768 px.** The table's own scroller now keeps its overflow to itself (`.scroller-x` — `overflow-x: auto` plus `contain: paint`, in `theme.css`); the table, its columns and every width above and below are untouched. Confirmed by scrolling the page right and measuring: `scrollX` 6 → **0**.
