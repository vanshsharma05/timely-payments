Nothing else touched; no business rule, permission, schema or backend change; the existing primitives (`Primitives.tsx` tokens, `Badge`, `Button`, `EmptyState`) reused.

## Phase 4, batch 2 — the Manager's Today and Reports (2026-09-17, deployed 22:21 IST)

Scope: the company dashboard (Today for Manager/Admin), Reports, the team table, the ageing view, the drill from a number to its accounts. No financial formula, business rule, permission, schema or backend change; every count and amount is computed by the same code as before (`boxMetrics`, `crmPerformanceStats`, `portfolioAgeing`, `overdueAgeing`).

**What was confusing for a manager, found at 1366×768 with the live data (642 accounts with dues):**

- **Reports was a different app.** Four pink "instant" cards, a "Portfolio Overview & Ageing Summary" block with six tiles, then rainbow category chips — three summaries of the same numbers in a colour language nothing else uses, before the list started. The list itself rendered all 642 rows at once (a 58,000 px page).
- **Different words for the same thing.** The book says *Upcoming*, *No follow-up*, *Follow up*; Reports said *Future*, *No Date*, *Not Set*, *Update*, "Select CRM Owner:", ">90d Report", "Instant Report of >135d".
- **A separate search.** Reports had its own box; the app bar's term (Ctrl+K) did nothing there.
- **Today's numbers were dead ends.** The team table sat at the bottom of a long page with no way from a person's *Unattended 50* to those fifty accounts; the ageing bands were figures only; the worklist subtitle said "open it in the customer book" while the cards opened Reports.
- **Filters leaked.** Arriving in Reports from a card kept whatever ageing band or person was chosen last time, so the list under "Overdue" could silently be "Overdue · 91–135 days · Savia".

**What changed:**

- **Reports, top to bottom, in the book's chrome:** one filter card (CRM owner · With dues/Settled/All · Search, with *AI summary* and *Excel* as quiet buttons) → four attention tiles (Overdue, Due today, No follow-up, Bad debt — each is the filter it names; press it, the list is that list; press again, back to All) → one compact ageing card (the bar, then Outstanding + timely score and the four bands, each a filter) → the list, headed "*N* accounts", with the follow-up-state chips (All · Overdue · Due today · Upcoming · No follow-up · Unattended · Completed · Bad debt) and the ageing chips (All ageing · 1–45d · 46–90d · 91–135d · >135d · >90d total) in the book's colours. *Reset* clears state, ageing and search together. A *Needs attention* chip appears only while the banner's list is open, so a manager always sees which list they are looking at.
- **The rows:** *Customer · Balance · Ageing (bar; figures at xl) · Over 90 days · Follow-up / Status (the same `StatusBadge` and the same "3d overdue · 16 Sept" wording as the book, "₹11,953 expected") · CRM owner · Last note (xl) · WhatsApp + **Follow up***. Fifty rows at a time, *Show more — n left*. The name opens the account, as in the book. Fits 1024 px without sideways scroll.
- **One search:** the Reports box and the app bar's edit the same term; Ctrl+K focuses it here too.
- **Today, in the order a manager reads it:** Worklist (Overdue · Due today · No follow-up · Upcoming, + the bad-debt strip) → **Team** → Portfolio ageing → Cheques / Commitments. The team table is most-unattended first; its columns are *Accounts with dues · Completed · Due today · Overdue · Unattended · Timely score* (hover for what each means); a CRM's name and every count is a button that opens Reports on that person and that list (Accounts → all, Completed → completed, Due today → today, Overdue → overdue, Unattended → overdue-or-no-follow-up). A collector's row stays plain numbers: Reports filters by CRM owner and has no filter for accounts handed to a collector. Each ageing band and the *Past 45 / Past 90 days* figures open Reports on that band.
- **`openReport(...)`** in `App.tsx` is the one way from Today into Reports: it sets the person, the state and the band together (unset = none), so nothing from an earlier visit carries over; the banner's *Show them* goes through it too.
- **`unattended`** joined `FollowUpCategoryFilter` (overdue or no follow-up, never bad debt) so the team table's column and the Reports chip are the same set; the numbers agree (Savia: Unattended 50 = Overdue 41 + No follow-up 9).
- **Vocabulary** the same in Today, the team table, Reports and the book; `followUpWhen` moved to `components/ui/format.ts` so both views read it from one place; the book's "All Ageing" chip is "All ageing".

**Owner's decisions (same day):** no collector filter in Reports yet (T50 stays documented); the team table stays most-unattended first; and **T51 fixed** — a CRM's *Accounts with dues* count opens Reports on *With dues, not bad debt (n)*, a chip shown only while that list is open, so it lists exactly the accounts it counted; the "+n bad debt" line beneath the count is unchanged and the *Bad debt* chip/tile still carry them. For the same reason Reports' *Completed* no longer includes a defaulter (they were already out of every other follow-up list; production has 0 such accounts today). The name, which is not a count, opens the person's whole book with the *Bad debt* badges. The "Timely score" formula is unchanged and now has a tooltip. The AI summary and Excel export are unchanged.

**Visual QA (local, every write aborted) at 1366×768, 1440×900, 1024×768:** no horizontal overflow on Today or Reports; the Reports table fits without sideways scroll at all three (the ageing figures and *Last note* fold below xl); 50 rows rendered; the team table's name → Reports on that person; *Unattended 50* → Reports "50 accounts" with the *Unattended* chip pressed and *Savia* selected; the 91–135 band → the band and the chip both pressed, 191 accounts; the *Overdue* card → *Overdue* chip with no stale person or band; *Show them* → *Needs attention (135)*; the tiles and chips agree in both directions; the app bar's term narrows Reports and Reports' box shows it; Ctrl+K focuses the search on Reports; *Follow up* from a Reports row opens the dialog inside the viewport. 0 findings after three fixes (the second Reports render site was not wired to the new props; the ageing column was fixed-width at 1024; the banner carried a stale band).

## Phase 4, batch 3 — the cheque register and its dialog (2026-09-17, deployed 23:21 IST)

Scope: the PDC cheques screen (`PdcChequesView`), the record/edit cheque dialog (`PdcModal`), the cheque badge in the follow-up dialog's Cheques fold. No cheque rule changed: where a cheque stands is still `chequeState()` from its date and status; the four stored statuses, the roles that may touch a cheque, the row-level policies and the table are untouched. Two confirmed small bugs fixed on the way (below).

**What was confusing, found at 1366×768 with the live register (150 cheques, 6 due today):**

- **Six cards filled the first screen** — five in a row and the sixth ("Bounced") alone on a second row — before the list started; the page was 11,190 px because all 150 rows rendered at once.
- **The list opened on August's cleared cheques.** Sorted by date ascending, the ninety cleared cheques came first and today's six were fifty rows down.
- **Every row carried three toggles**, with "✓ Clear" lit green on all ninety cleared rows and a "Cleared" badge beside it saying the same thing; the delete bin sat on every row.
- **Five spellings for six states**: cards said "Today's Bank Presentation / Date passed, not banked / Upcoming PDCs / Cheques on Hold / Cleared in Bank / Bounced / Returned"; chips said "Today's Due / Pending / On Hold"; badges said "Due Today / Overdue / Pending"; the follow-up dialog showed the raw stored word "Pending".
- **"Upcoming PDCs" counted 55 and listed 61** — the card counted the computed *upcoming* state but its chip filtered on the stored status *Pending*, which also covers the ones due today. (Same class of mismatch as T51 in Reports.)
- **Two headings** ("Post-dated cheques" from the shell, then "PDC Cheques Management" inside), a 4,027-option customer select, and a "Due Date" select beside status chips that partly overlapped it.
- **The dialog guessed**: the customer select defaulted silently to the first account alphabetically (3 BROTHERS), the bank to HDFC, the date to fifteen days out — three wrong values waiting for a hurried Save. Its header was a solid accent block unlike every other dialog.
- **A cheque had no way back to its customer** — `onOpenCustomerFollowUp` was never passed, so the "Follow-up →" link never rendered.
- **A refused status change** was a toast only; the row looked saved.

**What changed:**

- **One vocabulary** (`components/ui/ChequeState.tsx`): *Date passed · Due today · Bounced · On hold · Upcoming · Cleared*, each with one line saying what it means (tooltips), used by the strip, the badges, the list header, the delete question, the Excel export ("Where it stands" column added beside the stored "Status") and the follow-up dialog's Cheques fold. `chequeWhen()` in `ui/format.ts` says when a cheque is dated the way the book says when a follow-up is due ("Today", "in 4d · 21 Sept", "3d ago · 14 Sept").
- **The register, top to bottom, in the book's chrome:** one filter card (Find a cheque · CRM owner · Bank · Cheque date · Excel · **+ Record a cheque**) → one strip of seven cells (*In hand* and the six states, count and amount, each the filter it names; press again for all; the three that need attention go red when non-zero) → the list headed "*N* cheques · ₹X · state", a *Reset*, and a customer chip when arriving from an account.
- **Order:** in hand first by date (date passed at the very top, then today, then upcoming), then bounced, on hold, and cleared newest first (`sortCheques`). Fifty rows at a time, *Show more — n left*.
- **Rows:** *Customer (name opens the account; O/S beneath) · Cheque (#number, bank · note) · Amount · Status · dated (badge + "Today" / "3d ago · 14 Sept" / the date) · CRM owner (xl) · Actions*. A coloured left edge per state. Actions fit the state: in hand → *Clear · Hold · Bounce*; on hold → *Clear · Release · Bounce*; bounced → *Clear · Back to pending*; cleared → a quiet *Undo*. Edit always; delete only on hover or keyboard focus, and it asks.
- **Deleting asks properly** (`components/ui/ConfirmDialog.tsx`): "Delete this cheque? #405987 · Punjab National Bank · ₹46,006 · dated 17 Sept 2026 · KRISHNA PACKERS. It leaves the register for everyone and cannot be undone. A cheque that was returned should be marked bounced, not deleted." Cancel first and focused; the red button says *Delete cheque*; Esc is no. A selection's delete names the total and the first three numbers.
- **The dialog guesses nothing:** *Record a cheque* / *Edit cheque #405987* in the same header as the other dialogs. The customer is found by typing (name, contact, phone; eight matches; Enter takes the first) and shown as a line with balance, contact and owner, with *Change* — fixed when opened from an account. Bank is typed with suggestions from the register first, then the common banks. *Dated* is the date on the cheque, with "in 15d · 2 Oct" under it and a red "it will show as Date passed" when it has gone. *Where it stands* is a four-way segmented control with one line under it saying what the choice means. *Received on* defaults to today. A number already recorded for that customer from that bank is pointed out (not blocked). The button says *Record cheque* / *Save changes*. On a phone the sheet is the screen with the buttons pinned.
- **Failure and empty states:** a refused change is said on the row ("Not saved, retrying" with the reason in the tooltip) as well as in the header; *Loading the register…* until the first read; "No cheques recorded yet" vs "No cheques match" with *Reset filters*.
- **The name opens the account** — both register render sites now pass `handleOpenFollowUp`.

**Two small confirmed bugs fixed:** (1) the "Upcoming" chip/card mismatch above — every filter key is now a computed state; (2) pressing *Clear* on an already-cleared cheque rewrote `clearedDate` to now and saved the row — `handleUpdatePdcStatus` ignores a press for the state the cheque is already in. **One data-honesty change to say out loud:** editing a cheque used to overwrite *added by* with the editor's name; it now keeps the original adder.

**Not changed, by design:** the `Pending/Hold/Cleared/Bounced` stored statuses and the derivation of due/date-passed/upcoming from the date; who may manage cheques; bulk actions (the same four states + delete, now behind the named question); the Excel export's columns (one added); the manager's Today card ("Cheques to present today"); the daily email. There is no "deposited" state in the model — a cheque goes from in hand straight to cleared or bounced. **Owner's decisions (same day):** no Deposited/Presented state in this release — the cheque model stays as it is (a later feature if needed, T54); the silent defaults stay removed (no HDFC, no +15 days: the person enters the real bank and date); the register order stays as built; no sort control.
